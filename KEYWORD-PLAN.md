# Keyword plan

Prepared on 5 November 2026 from the 18 supplied Google Keyword Planner exports.

The exports cover 1 October 2025 to 30 September 2026 and contain 3,500 rows, representing 3,011 distinct phrases. The currency is INR. Location and Search Network targeting are not stated in these files.

Selections use service relevance, the intent of the page and reported demand. Job searches, AI assistant software, other company names and services outside the current scope were excluded. Blank search volume is shown as unavailable. Close variants can share search-volume estimates, so these figures should be considered individually.

The Competition field measures Google Ads advertiser competition. These exports do not provide organic keyword difficulty.

## Counting method

Combined density is the total non-overlapping mentions of the selected phrases divided by the number of editorial words, multiplied by 100. Matching is case-insensitive, with normal possessive forms included. The longest matching phrase is counted once. The count includes headings, body copy, tables and expandable FAQs. Shared navigation, the shared banner, the footer, elements marked aria-hidden and inactive sample tabs are excluded.

The agreed target is approximately 2% combined across the selected phrases. The 5 November figures below describe the original 19-page baseline. The 10 November proofreading pass measured 1.96%–2.03% combined across 29 content pages, with three or four selected phrases on each page. The 404 error page has no keyword targets.

## Heading and title widths

The website uses Calibri with a 10 pt minimum. Standard desktop heading sizes are H1 66.6667px, H2 46.6667px and H3 24.6667px. Tablet sizes are H1 54.6667px and H2 40.6667px. Standard mobile sizes are 37.6667px, 32.6667px and 22.6667px. The home hero, industry hero, cards and footer banner have sizes suited to their layouts.

Page-title widths below were measured using Arial at 20px. These are desktop preview measurements. Google can choose a different title or truncate it to fit the user’s device. All page titles are below the working 580px preview budget. Meta descriptions are below a 920px preview budget in Arial at 14px.

Desktop and 390px mobile checks covered all 19 content pages with no horizontal overflow or clipped headings.
