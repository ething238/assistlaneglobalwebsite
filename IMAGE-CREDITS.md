## Editorial photos added for page coverage

Each scene below was generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. The 1280 px file and its `-720.webp` version are both stored in `dist/` and selected by the browser according to display size.

All ten prompts shared these instructions:

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: landscape editorial photo for the GlobalAlly virtual assistant website
Style and framing: premium but candid business editorial photography, landscape 3:2, primary subject and important objects inside the central 75% so a 16:10 crop works. Realistic hands and natural proportions, no posed stock-photo smiles. Subtle contrast, neutral navy and soft blue that matches a blue website.
Constraints: no readable text, names, logos, brands, watermarks, or sensitive information. No collages or borders.
```

The scene and subject instruction for each photo was:

- [client-support.webp](dist/client-support.webp) — A customer support specialist wearing a small headset checks a reply on a laptop, with a second colleague slightly blurred in the background. Side-on upper-body view, natural concentration, headset and hands clear, sunlit customer operations workspace.
- [editorial-review.webp](dist/editorial-review.webp) — An editorial coordinator compares a printed set of abstract colour and layout proofs with an open laptop, a pencil and a neatly stacked set of draft pages. Over-the-shoulder view of an editorial studio, distinct cobalt accents and natural window light.
- [data-review.webp](dist/data-review.webp) — A business operations analyst working at a laptop compares a printed table of abstract rows with a second screen of softly blurred data blocks, pen resting on a blue notebook. Oblique view across the desk, navy and muted cyan with warm daylight, quiet focused moment.
- [hiring-review.webp](dist/hiring-review.webp) — Two hiring coordinators meet across a conference table to review a shortlist, one turning an anonymous folder beside an open notebook and laptop. Medium wide candid framing, a modern small business office, blue and warm stone palette. No interviewee present.
- [property-operations.webp](dist/property-operations.webp) — A property coordinator at a bright desk compares a simplified floor plan and appointment notes beside a tablet and a keyring. Soft-focus residential buildings through the window, blue-grey surfaces and natural daylight, elegant editorial crop with the objects in clear view.
- [practice-intake.webp](dist/practice-intake.webp) — A medical practice receptionist in everyday professional clothes coordinates appointments at the front desk, with an open appointment notebook and laptop and an empty waiting area behind. Clean daylight, subtle navy and pale blue tones, no patients or private information.
- [field-operations.webp](dist/field-operations.webp) — A construction project coordinator in workwear checks a rolled site plan and a tablet at a site office desk. A hard hat and a high-visibility vest lie to one side, an out-of-focus construction site visible beyond the window. Daylight, grounded documentary realism.
- [matter-review.webp](dist/matter-review.webp) — A legal operations coordinator organises unmarked navy matter folders and checks a calendar on a laptop at a tidy desk. Medium close-up of hands and files in a contemporary legal office, textured paper and subdued blue-grey daylight. No courthouse imagery.
- [member-coordination.webp](dist/member-coordination.webp) — Two nonprofit event coordinators prepare plain lanyards, checklists and a laptop for a volunteer sign-in table in a bright community meeting room. Candid interaction, medium-wide view, modest realistic space, a muted blue palette with a little warm light.
- [access-review.webp](dist/access-review.webp) — Close-up of an operations lead reviewing access at a laptop with an unbranded small USB security key, closed notebook and pen. The screen shows only softly blurred abstract interface blocks. Clean desk, side-on composition, cool navy daylight, professional rather than dramatic.
